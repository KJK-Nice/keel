pg-2 replication lag

Runbook·Updated Oct 14 by you·prod-db-01prod-db-02

Symptoms

Replica pg-2 falls 10–20 MB behind, and the stream drops every 30 s with SSL SYSCALL error. It restarts from the same WAL segment, so it never catches up. Failover rules are in the Patroni docs.

Check

  1. Confirm what the leader sees for the replica.
  2. Compare sent_lsn with replay_lsn.
  3. If the gap grows, check the network path between the two hosts.
$ sudo -u postgres psql -xc "select client_addr, state,
    sent_lsn, replay_lsn, replay_lag from pg_stat_replication;"

Notes

  • The leader is pg-1 at 10.0.4.21.
  • Do not restart pg-2 during business hours.
  • TCP keepalives are set in /etc/patroni.yml.

Before you touch anything: if lag passes 500 MB, page the DB owner.
